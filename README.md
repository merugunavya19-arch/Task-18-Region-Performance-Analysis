# Task 18 – Region Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Sample Superstore dataset** to compare sales and profit performance across different regions. The analysis was performed using **Microsoft Excel and Power BI** to summarize regional performance and create visual comparisons.

## Objective

- Compare total sales across regions.
- Compare total profit across regions.
- Rank regions based on sales performance.
- Identify the highest and lowest performing regions.
- Create visualizations to present regional performance.

## Dataset

**Dataset:** Sample - Superstore.xlsx

- Rows: 9,994
- Columns: 21
- Sheet: `RawData`
- Region Column: `M`
- Sales Column: `R`
- Profit Column: `U`

## Methodology

1. Loaded the Sample Superstore dataset.
2. Selected the **Region**, **Sales**, and **Profit** columns.
3. Grouped the data by region.
4. Calculated total sales for each region.
5. Calculated total profit for each region.
6. Compared sales and profit across regions.
7. Ranked the regions based on sales performance.
8. Created bar charts to visualize regional sales and profit.
9. Created a Power BI dashboard titled **Region Performance**.

## Region Performance Summary

| Region | Total Sales | Total Profit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Central | ₹501,239.89 | ₹39,706.36 |
| East | ₹678,781.24 | ₹91,522.78 |
| South | ₹391,721.91 | ₹46,749.43 |
| West | ₹725,457.82 | ₹108,418.45 |

## Key Findings

1. **West** recorded the highest total sales of **₹725,457.82**.
2. **West** also generated the highest total profit of **₹108,418.45**.
3. **South** had the lowest total sales of **₹391,721.91**.
4. **Central** had the lowest total profit of **₹39,706.36**.
5. The comparison shows that the **West region was the strongest overall performer** in terms of both sales and profit.
